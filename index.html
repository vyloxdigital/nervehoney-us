Marshmallow Root

Tissue Comfort

Rich in mucilaginous compounds, Marshmallow Root forms a soothing protective layer around irritated nerve tissue, helping to reduce the sharp touch-sensitivity and discomfort associated with peripheral nerve issues.

Corydalis Powder

Discomfort Relief

Used for centuries in Eastern botanical traditions, Corydalis is studied for its ability to intercept pain signal transmission along nerve pathways, helping reduce the tingling, sharp, and burning sensations of nerve discomfort.

Prickly Pear

Circulation & Antioxidants

This vibrant cactus fruit delivers powerful antioxidant betalains that support peripheral circulation — the network of small blood vessels that feed your peripheral nerves. Better circulation means better nourishment for nerve tissue.

Muscle Relaxation

California Poppy Seed contains alkaloids that act on the nervous system to ease muscle tension and spasm — the involuntary tightening that often accompanies nerve pain. It supports a calmer, more comfortable neuromuscular state.
